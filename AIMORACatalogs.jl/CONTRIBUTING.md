# Catalog contribution rules

Every submitted asset must include:

- a stable identifier and equipment class;
- source provenance and an explicit redistribution licence;
- physical units in parameter names;
- common nameplate data and at least one study facet;
- validation against a public source or an independently calculated check.

Do not copy vendor catalogs, curves, or proprietary model files without documented redistribution permission.
